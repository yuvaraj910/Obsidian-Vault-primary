# Kaizen Day Workflow

> **Kaizen means continuous improvement.** Use each of the four monthly
> days off for a different major interest, while making Systems &
> Learning Optimization compulsory every time.

## Operating Rules

-   Plan around **four days off per month**, not four extra days every
    week.
-   Give each day off a different primary theme so that interests get
    deliberate time without competing every day.
-   **Systems & Learning Optimization is compulsory on all four Kaizen
    Days.**
-   Keep the primary theme flexible if work, exams, health, or urgent
    responsibilities require it.
-   End each session with a concrete output, not just browsing or
    watching tutorials.
-   Do not try to do every interest on every Kaizen Day.

## Monthly Rotation

### Kaizen Day 1 --- Build & Vibe Coding

**Primary focus:** Build useful software, automations, prototypes, or
small tools.

Suggested activities: - Pick one practical problem to solve. - Research
only what is needed to begin. - Build a working first version. - Test it
and record the next improvement.

**Possible deliverables:** a working feature, prototype, script,
automation, or small project.

### Kaizen Day 2 --- Business Models & Opportunities

**Primary focus:** Understand how useful products, businesses, and ideas
work.

Suggested activities: - Research a product or business model. - Identify
the audience, problem, competitors, monetization, costs, and
distribution. - Compare evidence rather than relying on assumptions. -
Identify one opportunity or improvement worth exploring.

**Possible deliverables:** a concise research note, comparison,
validated idea, or business-model breakdown.

### Kaizen Day 3 --- Electronics & Deep Technical Learning

**Primary focus:** Explore electronics, hardware, systems, or a
challenging technical concept.

Suggested activities: - Choose one question or technical topic. - Learn
the underlying principles. - Work through an experiment, simulation,
schematic, or practical example. - Record what worked and what remains
unclear.

**Possible deliverables:** an experiment, simulation, schematic,
technical note, or tested explanation.

### Kaizen Day 4 --- Content Creation & Finance

**Primary focus:** Develop creator skills and financial/business
understanding.

Suggested activities: - Research a content format, production technique,
or creator workflow. - Work on an outline, script, edit, or publishing
plan. - Alternatively, complete a focused finance or business
analysis. - Choose a tangible output for the session rather than trying
to do everything.

**Possible deliverables:** a video outline, script, content plan,
production experiment, budget analysis, or finance note.

## Compulsory Block --- Systems & Learning Optimization

**Do this on every Kaizen Day, regardless of the day's primary theme.**

Suggested duration: **2 hours**, adjustable to the available time.

### 1. Find the bottleneck

-   [ ] What slowed down learning or execution since the last review?
-   [ ] What caused confusion, distraction, repeated work, or friction?

### 2. Improve one system

-   [ ] Improve one part of the learning workflow, Obsidian
    organization, Codex workflow, project setup, or personal
    productivity system.
-   [ ] Prefer one useful improvement over a large redesign.

### 3. Test the improvement

-   [ ] Try the change on a real task.
-   [ ] Check whether it actually saves time, reduces friction, or
    improves clarity.

### 4. Record the result

-   [ ] What changed?
-   [ ] Did it help? What evidence supports that?
-   [ ] What should be adjusted or left alone?

### 5. Prepare the next week

-   [ ] Choose the main learning priority.
-   [ ] Choose the next concrete project task.
-   [ ] Identify one weakness to practice.
-   [ ] Make the first task easy to start.

**Rule:** Do not reorganize tools just to make them look better. Improve
the system when there is a real problem to solve.

## Suggested Day Structure

Use this as a flexible template for a full day off.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Time                    Block                   Purpose
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  8:30--10:30             Theme deep work 1       Research, learning, or
                                                  planning for the day's
                                                  theme

  10:45--12:45            Theme deep work 2       Build, analyze,
                                                  experiment, or create

  12:45--1:30             Break                   Lunch and rest

  1:30--3:30              Theme deep work 3       Continue the main task
                                                  and produce an output

  3:45--5:45              Theme deep work 4       Finish, test, refine,
                                                  or package the output

  5:45--6:30              Break                   Rest and reset

  6:30--8:30              **Systems & Learning    Improve one system and
                          Optimization ---        plan the next week
                          compulsory**            
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Treat the times as a template, not a reason to force work when tired or
when other responsibilities take priority.

## Definition of Done

A Kaizen Day is complete when: 1. A meaningful attempt was made at the
day's primary theme. 2. The compulsory Systems & Learning Optimization
block was completed. 3. At least one concrete output, tested
improvement, or clearly documented result exists. 4. The next week's
first task is clear.

A day does not have to be perfect to count. The purpose is steady,
measurable improvement across the month.
