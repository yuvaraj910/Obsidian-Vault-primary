- **Best Season:** **October through April** is the prime window. Post-monsoon humidity drops, leaving crisp, clear, cloudless skies. (Avoid the monsoon months of June through September due to heavy cloud cover and rain).

- **Best Lunar Phase:** Plan your trip around **new moon weekends** when moonlight doesn't wash out faint stars and the Milky Way. 

- **Peak Hours:** Deep-sky viewing and astrophotography are best **from midnight to 5:00 AM**, though introductory constellation sessions typically start around 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM
